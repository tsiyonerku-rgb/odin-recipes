Doro Wet recipe

Doro Wet is Ethiopian traditional food.

Ingredients:

  • chicken pieces
  • Niter Kibbeh (spiced clarified butter)
  • pepper
  • water
  • spicy
  • salt
  • onion
  • garlic

Instructions:

  1. first Mix and cook the onion with some pepper, garlic, cooking oil and water on stove.
  2. finally put the chicken pieces and serve for meal.
