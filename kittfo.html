Kittfo recipe

Kittfo is Ethiopian traditional food.

Ingredients:

  • Finely minced fresh beef
  • Niter Kibbeh (spiced clarified butter)
  • Mitmita (chili powder spice blend)

Instructions:

  1. Mix the minced beef with melted spiced butter and mitmita.
  2. Warm it slightly on low heat if preferred leb leb, or serve it raw.
